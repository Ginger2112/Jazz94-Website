Step 1

Logo concepts

Each concept shows the full logo on the page background, on dark ink and on the second brand colour, plus the small icon for the favicon, app icon and social avatar.

Step 2

Brand colour palettes

Each palette has a lead colour for buy buttons and sale moments, a second colour for big blocks, a pop colour for badges and stickers, and an ink and paper pair for text and backgrounds. The checks show how readable text is; 4.5:1 passes WCAG AA for normal text.

Step 3

Font pairings

A display face for the logo and big headlines, and a body face for menus, product names and checkout. Prices and order numbers use JetBrains Mono in every option. All fonts are free on Google Fonts.
